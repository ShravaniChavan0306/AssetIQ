# ASSET MANAGEMENT POLICY

## 1. Asset Allocation
Every company IT asset should be assigned to an employee using a recorded allocation. The employee ID and asset ID must be recorded.

## 2. Asset Return
When an employee returns an asset, the return date must be recorded. An allocation with no return date is considered active.

## 3. Asset Maintenance
Assets requiring repair should be recorded in the maintenance register. Open and In Progress maintenance records are considered active.

## 4. Asset Availability
An asset marked Available can be considered for a new allocation, subject to company approval.

## 5. Warranty
The warranty expiry date should be checked before arranging paid repairs. Warranty information must be verified against the asset record.

## 6. Asset Security
Employees should use company assets responsibly and report loss, damage or malfunction to the IT department.

## 7. Replacement
An asset replacement should be considered when repair is not practical, the asset is obsolete, or the maintenance team recommends replacement.

## 8. Asset Records
Asset IDs and employee IDs should be used to identify records accurately.

## 9. Allocation Approval
New asset allocations should be approved by the authorized IT department or asset management administrator before the asset is issued.

## 10. Overdue Asset Returns
Employees should return company assets when requested or when their employment or assigned responsibilities end. Delayed returns should be reported to the IT department.

## 11. Asset Transfer
An asset transferred from one employee to another must have its existing allocation updated and a new allocation recorded with the correct employee ID and asset ID.

## 12. Preventive Maintenance
IT equipment should be inspected periodically to identify potential hardware problems before they cause major failures.

## 13. Maintenance Priority
Critical issues affecting essential business operations should receive priority from the IT support team.

## 14. Asset Inspection
Assets should be inspected when issued, returned, or transferred to identify physical damage and verify their condition.

## 15. Asset Disposal
Damaged, obsolete, or unusable assets should be reviewed by the authorized department before disposal. Asset records should be updated after disposal approval.

## 16. Inventory Verification
The organization should periodically compare physical IT assets with the asset register to identify missing, misplaced, or incorrectly recorded assets.

## 17. Data Protection
Employees must protect company information stored on assigned devices. Sensitive company data should not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

## 18. Software Usage
Software installed on company assets should be used only for authorized purposes and must comply with applicable company licensing requirements.

---

**Note:** These are sample policies prepared for the AssetIQ academic project. Actual company policies may differ.
